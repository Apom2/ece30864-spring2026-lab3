1. Pros: Encourages team work, reduces waste and improves efficiency. Cons: Can get lost among all the tasks and scope of the project, Relies on communication heavily
2. Scrum Master is more like a project/team manager that helps keep the team moving and the group working together. The Product Owner focuses more on the actual product being worked on and making sure its working best.
3. Sprint Planning, which is the initial planning meeting, the Sprint Review, which takes place at the end of a sprint to demonstrate the project to those who need to see it. The sprint retrospective phase is how the team views that feedback and plans to improve itself.
4. An Epic is a large feature of a project that groups a lot of similar features in order to organize the most important parts of a project. A Story is a small piece of work that can be done to improve the project by a little. A Task is a specific piece of work that getting done will have known results and fixes to the project. A Bug represents different problems that can bbe very small that need to be solved to improve functionality of the project.
